title: Show Tree 0.3.0 beta
---
The beta that made qlcconsole a touring console: the same show can move
from room to room, keeping each venue's own rig and focus.

**Highlights**

- **Build and the Workbench:** scenes, collections and chasers edited as
  layers, shared with the Show Tree.
- **Venues:** a house rig, outputs and focus corrections for each room, and a
  clash check before you apply one.
- **Focus from the stage:** Quick focus, Venue focus and Focus this cue,
  with a controller.
- **Three views:** Classic QLC+, qlcconsole and Everything.
- **Eos import,** including special circuits and subs.
