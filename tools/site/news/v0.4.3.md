title: qlcconsole 0.4.3
---
A stable release that rolls in the unpublished 0.4.2 and fixes a freeze when
a playing scene is deleted.

**Highlights**

- **Follow spots framed from the performer:** Head & Shoulders, Waist Up or
  Full Body, opened and closed live from the controller's D-pad.
- **Shape the plot by hand:** stretch and turn trusses, pipes, walls and
  platforms right on the plot.
- **Save as Formation…** keeps a layer's Spread, Grouping and Cross settings
  for reuse.
- **WLEDs found on the network** and patched with a right-click.
- **Fits a small laptop:** the Lighting Studio's editors and dialogs now fit a
  1024 × 640 screen.
