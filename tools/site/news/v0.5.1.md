title: qlcconsole 0.5.1
---
A stability and room-setup release. A big show now opens quickly on
Windows, control surfaces gain an emergency stop of their own, and a
touring show can be set up in a new room with the house lights ready to use.

**Highlights**

- **Big shows open on Windows:** a 95-universe, 2,800-fixture show that took
  almost four minutes to open now takes 18 seconds, and it is faster on every
  machine.
- **An emergency stop on any control surface:** learn one button once, and it
  turns every light off from that desk, whatever mode you are in.
- **New Venue from a Show:** bring in a room's own show file and line the
  room up under your touring show, by the centre line and the upstage drape.
- **House lights ready to run:** fill the house roles in one pass and get six
  standard cues: House to half, House out, House full, Stage up, Stage out
  and Work lights.
- **A hit row on the Run tab:** a button under GO for each hit that is armed
  right now, with the house hits at the end.
