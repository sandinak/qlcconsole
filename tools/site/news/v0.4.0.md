title: Show Tree 0.4.0 beta
---
A beta that brings the "Run the show" milestone and most of "The show
explains itself". It also restores QLC+ 4 habits for people coming from
QLC+.

**Highlights**

- **Run the show from a game controller:** GO, fire hits and switch roles
  from the pad.
- **Walk the show from the stage:** step through cues while standing where
  the audience will see them.
- **Teaching mode** for classrooms, with a GO log and glossary pop-ups.
- **Effects that explain themselves:** see where an effect came from, run it
  once or N times, and stack movement with intensity.
- **One cue list per show,** with song sections.
