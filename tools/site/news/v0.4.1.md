The first stable release of the 0.4 line. It hardens what 0.4.0 introduced,
and a save that can't be written in full no longer costs you the show.

**Highlights**

- **Your units everywhere:** every length follows Settings ▸ Units, in feet
  and inches by default.
- **Release and Untrack:** two clear ways to end a tracked layer.
- **Copy, paste and duplicate** cue lists and productions, and keep notes
  beside the Show Tree.
- **Steam Deck or gamepad remote** for running and focusing from anywhere.
- **Smarter effects:** the picker lists only effects that run on your
  fixtures.
