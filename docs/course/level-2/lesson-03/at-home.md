# At home — Lesson 2.3

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-3-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-3-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. The other side answers. Click **1.0.5 Bridge**, then **+ Cue**, type
   `Response`: **1.0.6 Response**. Set its **Mode** to **Block**.
3. Drag **Truss Sides** (All → Group sets) onto the dashed box, then
   **Blue** and **Full** onto its row.
4. Right-click its layer in the tree → **Add child layer** → **Trusses
   SL**, and drop **Amber** on the child: the mirror of the bridge.
5. Drag **Steps** onto the dashed box with **Blue** and **25%**, then hold
   **⇧** and drag **DS** onto the Steps row; drop **White** and **Full**
   on the child. The front row answers the top row.
6. Save it as `Lesson2-3-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in, or hand in
   a screenshot of the Rig Overview.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

1. Draw our stage from above: the three riser rows and the trusses on
   each side.
2. A bridge in four lines: SR sings, SL answers, the top row sings, all
   together. For each line, colour in which part of the stage is lit and
   how (colour, bright or dim).
3. Which line is symmetric, and which aren't? In a sentence, say what
   breaking the symmetry does for the audience.
