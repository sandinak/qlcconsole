## 2. The bridge: call and response

- SR sings, SL answers, the top row takes the line.
- *Ask:* "How does the audience know who to watch?" (The light tells
  them, Lesson 0.4.)

## 3. Symmetry

- Both sides the same: balanced, calm, formal.
- Break it, and the eye goes to the difference.
- Picture: `rig-bridge-sides.png` → `rig-bridge-split.png`.

## 4. A group set

- One name for several groups: **Truss Sides** = Trusses SR + SL.
- One layer: one look on both sides, set once.
- Picture: `group-set.png`.

## 5. A child layer

- A layer on part of its parent: one member of a set, a group inside it.
- It sets only what it sets; the parent lights the rest.
- A set can't be split; give one side a child.

## 6. Pick out a row

- Steps dim blue; **↳ US** white and full.
- ⇧ as you drop: a child layer.
