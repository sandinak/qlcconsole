# Lesson 2.3 — Parts of a group

The bridge is a call and response: one side of the choir sings, the
other answers, then the top row takes the line. Students light the two
truss sides as one **group set** (symmetry), give one side its own
colour with a **child layer**, and pick out the top row of singers with
a child layer on the Steps.
