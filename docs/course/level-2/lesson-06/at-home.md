# At home — Lesson 2.6

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-6-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-6-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. Add a fourth hit for an encore: right-click **Hits** → **+ Hit**,
   name it, and build it as you would a cue (a group, a colour, a level).
3. Right-click **Hits** → **Make VC page** again: the page gets a button
   for the new hit, and the three you had stay where they are.
4. Click **Operate**, play the song in the **Run** tab, and fire your
   hits from the **Virtual Console** while it runs.
5. Save as `Lesson2-6-Home-yourname.qlcc` (back in **Design** first) and
   bring it in with a sentence on when you'd use your new hit.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

1. Make four hit cards for a vamp you know: for each, a name (what it
   does), the lights, and the moment you'd fire it.
2. A partner plays or sings a vamp; you "fire" the cards by holding them
   up on the beat. Swap.
3. Which hit did the most with the least? Why?
