## The task

1. **Choose the song.** Your teacher assigns one, or your group proposes
   one for approval.
2. **Map it:** sections, what the audience feels at each change, builds,
   hits, the button.
3. **Design it on paper:** a cue sheet of about 8–15 cues, plus your
   hits.

   | Cue | When | Feel | Lights (group: colour, level, aim, effect) | Mode / time |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1.0.0 | | | | |
   | | | | | |

4. **Program it** from `lesson-1-1-start.qlcc`: **+ Production** (in the header, or under **More ▾**)
   named for your group, **+ Cue list** named for the song, then your
   cues. It must have:
   - at least one **Block** cue, and **Release** or **Cue-only** where
     something mustn't carry on;
   - at least one **palette of the song's own**, named for its job, in a
     folder for the song;
   - at least one **effect**;
   - at least one **hit** on a Virtual Console button.
5. **Save** as `Level2-check-group.qlcc`.
6. **Run it live** while the song plays: one of you calls as SM, another
   is on the board, and you fire a hit at least once.
7. **Talk for two minutes:** what you wanted the audience to feel, and
   how the light did it.

Everything you need is in Lessons 2.1–2.7, on top of Level 1.

## How it's marked

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Design** | Cues with no reasons | Some cues have a reason | Every cue has a reason; a build to the peak and a button | A story across the song; contrast, holding back, every quality chosen on purpose |
| **Programming** | Doesn't play, or a required item is missing | Differs from the sheet, or things carry on by accident | Plays the sheet; all four required items, where they belong | Clean; each tool doing a job; faces lit throughout |
| **Operation** | Cues missed or very late | Some early or late | Every cue on its call; the hit on the music | Clean calls, GOs and Sub GOs; a mistake recovered calmly |
| **Teamwork** | One person did it | Roles shared unevenly | Everyone had a role | Everyone can explain every cue and why |

**Pass:** 3 or more in every row. Below 3 in a row, you get notes on what
to work on, then run it again.
