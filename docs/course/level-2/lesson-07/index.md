# Lesson 2.7 — Sub-cues: stomps inside a cue

The choir stomps on the chorus's last line: a hit of light on the stomp,
gone half a second later, while the chorus goes on. Students give the
chorus **sub-cues**: one taken with **Sub GO**, one that follows by
itself. Then the [Level 2 check](level-check.md): one full song, designed
and programmed from a cue sheet.
