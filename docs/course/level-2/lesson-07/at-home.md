# At home — Lesson 2.7

Do **one** of these, and start on your [Level 2 check](level-check.md)
cue sheet.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-7-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-7-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. In the **Run** tab, click **1.0.2 Chorus** to load it and press **GO**.
3. Press **Sub GO** on the beat of a song you know (clap along), and
   watch the stomp and its auto-follow. Do it again: can you land it on
   the beat every time?
4. In the Show Tree, click **1.0.2.3 Stomp Out** and change its wait
   (the box beside **Auto-follow after**) to `0.25`. Which suits a stomp
   better?
5. Save as `Lesson2-7-Home-yourname.qlcc`.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

1. Pick a chorus you know with stomps, claps or stings in it.
2. Write the chorus as one cue, then its sub-cues: for each, the moment,
   the lights, and **Sub GO** or **auto** (and how long after).
3. Which sub-cues need the op's timing (Sub GO), and which can follow by
   themselves? Why?
