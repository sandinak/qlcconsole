## 2. A sting inside a section

- Stomps, claps, stings: accents inside the chorus (Lesson 0.6).
- The chorus doesn't end; the stomp is on top of it.

## 3. Sub-cues

- 1.0.2.1, 1.0.2.2… under the chorus.
- **GO** goes on to the next cue; **Sub GO** steps through the sub-cues.
- The first plays with GO.

## 4. Advance

- **Sub GO**: waits for the op.
- **Auto-follow after**: goes on by itself.
- Picture: `stomp-out.png`.

## 5. Cue-only, by design

- What the sub-cues change doesn't pass on: the next cue starts from the
  chorus (Lesson 2.1).
