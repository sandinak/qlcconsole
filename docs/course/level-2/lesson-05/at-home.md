# At home — Lesson 2.5

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-5-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-5-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. Click **1.0.7 Fan**, then its **Truss-Spots** row.
3. On the **Spread** row, click **⋯ → 0**: every beam on the middle of the
   line. Then **⋯ → +2.5**: the fan squeezed into the middle half.
4. On the **Grouping** row, try **⋯ → Pairs**. Watch the order strip and
   the plan view under Position.
5. Choose the fan you like best for the moment before the button, save
   as `Lesson2-5-Home-yourname.qlcc`, and write one sentence on why.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

1. Draw our stage from above: the four truss spots on the trusses, and a
   line across the CS riser row.
2. Draw the beams at Spread +5 (spread end to end), 0 (all on the
   centre) and −5 (crossed). Number the lights 1–4 from stage right.
3. Which would you use for a dramatic, symmetric ending, and why?
