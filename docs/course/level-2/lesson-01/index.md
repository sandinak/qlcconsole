# Lesson 2.1 — Tracking and Block

A cue list only writes changes: whatever a cue doesn't change stays on.
Students read what tracks into a cue, let the verse's colour go at the
chorus with **Release**, and add a one-beat flash that doesn't carry
into the scream (**Cue-only**). The Solo from Lesson 1.3 is the **Block**
cue: a hard start.
