## 2. A cue list writes only changes

- Three cue cards: cyc blue; step tops lavender + front light; steps
  magenta.
- *Ask:* "In cue 3, what's on stage?" (All of it.)
- That's **tracking**: a light keeps its state until a cue changes it.

## 3. Reading the tree

- Grey ⤳ rows: tracked in, and from which cue.
- **S** sets · **T** tracks in · **R** releases · **M** mode (B, C).
- Picture: `chorus-tracking.png`.

## 4. On the cue's page

- **Tracked in**: each grey layer and its values.
- **Next cue receives**: what the next cue starts from.
- Picture: `chorus-receives.png`.

## 5. The director's note

- "The lavender step tops muddy the chorus."
- One look per idea (Lesson 0.5): the verse's colour belongs to the verse.

## 6. Release

- Ticked on a layer: it leaves when its cue is left.
- Back to the **base cue**, else dark.
- Pictures: `release.png`, `rig-chorus-tracked.png` → `rig-chorus-released.png`.

## 7. A flash for one beat

- A sting on the last word (Lesson 0.6): there and gone.
- **Cue-only**: its changes don't pass on.
- Pictures: `flash-cue.png`, `rig-flash.png`.

## 8. Block, Track, Cue-only

| Mode | Starts from | Passes on |
|---|---|---|
| Track | the cue before | everything on stage |
| Block | nothing | everything on stage |
| Cue-only | the cue before | what it started from |

- The Solo is Block: a hard start.
