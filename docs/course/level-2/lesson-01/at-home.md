# At home — Lesson 2.1

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-1-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-1-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. After the Solo the choir comes back in for the last chorus. Add
   **1.0.5 Final Chorus** after 1.0.4 Solo.
3. Look at its grey rows first: with the list's default mode (Track) it
   starts from the Solo, so it has the two spots and nothing else.
4. Make it a **Block** cue, then build the chorus look again: **Steps**
   Magenta, Full; **TrussC-RGB** Blue, Full; **AT-W-ALL** Warm White, Full,
   aimed at **Center**. Add one thing the first chorus didn't have, to
   make the last one bigger.
5. Save it as `Lesson2-1-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in, or hand in a
   screenshot of the Show Tree with the Final Chorus selected.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

Here is a short cue sheet. For each cue, write which layers are on in it
(its own, and what tracks in), then answer the questions.

| Cue | Sets |
|---|---|
| 1 Intro | Cyc: Blue, Full |
| 2 Verse | Step tops: Lavender, 40% · Front: Warm White, Full |
| 3 Chorus | Steps: Magenta, Full |
| 4 Stomp | Step tops: White, Full |
| 5 Solo | One spot: White, Full |

1. Which layers are on in cue 3? In cue 5?
2. Cue 4 is a one-beat flash. Which mode should it have, and what goes
   wrong in cue 5 if it doesn't?
3. Which cue should be a Block cue, and why?
4. Where would you tick **Release**, so the verse's lavender doesn't
   show in the chorus?
