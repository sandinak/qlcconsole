# At home — Lesson 2.4

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Download [`lesson-2-4-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-2-4-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**) and open it.
2. Click **1.0.6 Dance Break**, then its **Steps** row.
3. On the **Dimmer effect** row, click **⋯ → Its own colour / dimmer →
   Amber**. Watch the steps in the Rig Overview.
4. Then **⋯ → Its own colour / dimmer → None of its own**: the sparkle
   in the steps' own colour.
5. Which of the three (white, amber, none) suits the dance break best,
   and why? Set it back to that one, save as
   `Lesson2-4-Home-yourname.qlcc`, and bring it in with your answer.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook)

Plan the effects for a dance break you know (or ours).

1. List the lights on our stage. For each, write **still** or **moves**,
   and if it moves, how: a sparkle, a wave, a chase, a pulse.
2. Which lights light the dancers' faces and bodies? Keep them still and
   say why.
3. How fast should each effect be against the music: on the beat, twice
   as fast, half as fast? Why?
