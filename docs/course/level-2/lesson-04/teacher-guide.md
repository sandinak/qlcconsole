# Lesson 2.4 — Effects: sparkle and wave

**Teacher guide** · Level 2, lesson 4 · 50 minutes

**Give students:** <https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/2-4/> (the show file, the steps, the exit ticket and the at-home task; nothing else)

<img src="../../images/qr/class-2-4.svg" width="120" alt="QR code for https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/2-4/">

**Builds on:** Lessons [0.2](../../level-0/lesson-02/teacher-guide.md), [0.4](../../level-0/lesson-04/teacher-guide.md), [0.6](../../level-0/lesson-06/teacher-guide.md), and Lesson [2.2](../lesson-02/teacher-guide.md)'s song folder. Open with the handout's *Why this matters* before any clicks.

## Objectives

Students can:

1. say when an effect serves a song (energy, rhythm, a peak) and when it
   pulls focus, and keep effects on the scenery, not on faces;
2. make an **effect palette** (+ Palette → Effect, the picker, a name) in
   the song's folder and put it on a layer;
3. give an effect **its own colour**.

## 1. Design idea (10 min)

1. Ask: *"When does a show choir number use the most moving light?"*
   (The dance break, the final chorus, the button.) That's Lesson 0.6:
   energy builds to a peak, and effects are one of the ways light
   builds.
2. Movement is the fourth quality of light (Lesson 0.2), and the eye
   goes to it (Lesson 0.4). So an effect is a strong tool, and a
   dangerous one: under a ballad or a solo it pulls the eye off the
   singer.
3. **Eye candy vs light on people.** Sparkle on the risers, a wave on the
   trusses, a chase on the cyc: scenery that moves with the music. The
   singers' faces and bodies still need steady light. If an effect
   leaves a dancer in the dark, the audience misses the choreography.
4. Optional: the two clips. Which effects helped the number, and which
   distracted?

**Key idea to land:** effects follow the music's energy, live on the
scenery, and never cost the audience the performers.

## 2. Teacher demo (10 min)

On the projector:

1. Make **1.0.6 Dance Break** (Block) with the three still layers (steps
   1–6). Point out: nothing moves.
2. Click the song's folder in the Library, **+ Palette → Effect**. If the
   picker isn't open, **Change effect…**. Search `Sparkle`, pick
   **Sparkle · Dimmer**, **Done**. Name it `Step Sparkle`.
3. Leave the drop, its own colour and the wave for the students.

## 3. Hands-on (20 min)

- Groups of 2–3 with **Driver / Navigator / Designer** roles; switch after
  each part of the handout.
- Students without a laptop: the **design station**, the paper at-home
  task (B): an effects plan for a dance break.

### What to watch for

| You see | Likely cause | Say |
|---|---|---|
| The picker finds nothing | The search box still holds the last search | "Clear the search box first." |
| The palette is named *New Effect*, or after another effect | Return not pressed in Name (the name can start as the last effect's) | "Type the name you want and press Return." |
| No *Its own colour* in the menu | They opened ⋯ on the Colour row | "Which row is the effect?" |
| The whole stage is moving | Effects dropped on every layer | "Which lights are people, which are scenery?" |

## 4. Check for understanding (while circulating)

- "Why are the step tops still?" (*Contrast: something has to stay still
  for the movement to read; and they're the singers' uplight.*)
- "What would happen if the Truss Wave went in the verse?" (*It would
  pull the eye off the story.*)

## 5. Share (5 min)

Two groups play 1.0.5 Bridge → 1.0.6 Dance Break on the projector. Does

## Extension (fast groups)

From the handout: the Truss Wave on a **speed master** (the Look
Editor's **Speed: Main Speed**); the footer shows **Main Speed 120**.

Going further, for an op who busks: the dance break's speed belongs on a
fader, not in a menu. On the **Virtual Console**, a slider in **Speed
master** mode moves Main Speed's rate; a knob in **Effect** mode on **The
current effect · Speed** moves whichever effect started last, so the
same knob serves every song. Its value is live and goes when the effect
stops; **Store** keeps it. See
[Speed masters](https://github.com/sandinak/qlcconsole/blob/main/docs/features/effects.md#speed-masters) and
[Effect controls](https://github.com/sandinak/qlcconsole/blob/main/docs/features/effects.md#effect-controls-on-the-virtual-console).

## Answer key

- **Exit ticket:** 1 — the dance break is the energy peak (Lesson 0.6):
  moving light matches the music and builds; in the verse, movement
  would pull the eye off the singer and the story (Lesson 0.4). 2 — the
  sparkle on the steps and the wave on the truss boxes are eye candy;
  lighting the performers (faces, bodies, the choreography) must never
  be given up for it. 3 — it gives the effect a colour of its own,
  inside the effect only: the sparkle glints white while the steps stay
  in the chorus colour. 4 — the song's effects sit with its colours in
  one place, and a name by job (*Step Sparkle*, *Truss Wave*) says where
  it's used and stays true if the effect is changed.
- **Part 1 question:** the steps and the truss boxes move (scenery, the
  energy); the step tops stay still, so the movement has something to
  read against.
- **At home A:** any answer with a reason: white glints read as
  glitter, amber warms the violet, none keeps it all one colour.

## How this lesson was checked

Every step of the handout is run by the course test (`ui/test/courseshow`,
`levelTwoWithTheMouse`, `lessonTwoFourExtension`) on the real app,
offscreen, with the effect scripts the app ships with: the drops and
their hints, **+ Palette → Effect** with the song folder selected (the
palette is filed there), the effect picker (**Change effect…**, the
search, the click on *Sparkle · Dimmer* and *Rainbow*, **Done**), the
Look Editor's Name, **⋯ → Its own colour / dimmer**, and what the rig
sends: the steps still, then twinkling, with white pixels among them;
the truss boxes still, then a moving wave; the step tops still
throughout. The extension's Speed and the footer's **Main Speed 120**,
and the at-home Amber and *None of its own*, are checked the same way.

**Not in this lesson, and why:** the course plan also named a chase and
an effect dropped on the cue, across the whole stage. On qlcconsole
0.3.x, an effect that spans several layers (dropped on the cue, or one
palette on two layers) didn't move the truss boxes, and dimmer effects
(Sparkle, Dimmer Phaser) don't move them at all; a Color Chase across
the stage's hundreds of pixels crawls. Until that's fixed, the lesson
keeps each effect on one layer. See the
[course plan](https://github.com/sandinak/qlcconsole/blob/main/docs/course/COURSE_PLAN.md#found-writing-level-2-on-030).
