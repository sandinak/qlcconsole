# Lesson 2.4 — Effects: sparkle and wave

The dance break is the song's energy peak, and the light moves with it.
Students make two effect palettes of the song's own: a **sparkle** on the
pixel strips of the steps, with white glints of its own colour, and a
**wave** of blue across the truss boxes, while the risers' tops stay
still.
