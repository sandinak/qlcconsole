# Lesson 2.2 — Palettes: change once, change everywhere

A song's colours are a set the designer chooses, and the director's notes
come late. Students give the song a palette of its own, named for its job
(**Chorus Colour**) and filed in a folder for the song, use it on the
chorus, then take the note "less pink" with one change that every cue
follows.
