## Where the menus are

- **Mac:** at the top of the screen, while qlcconsole's window is in
  front. The first menu, **qlcconsole**, holds **Settings…**, **About**
  and **Quit**.
- **Windows and Linux:** at the top of the qlcconsole window: **File,
  Edit, View, Fixtures, Control, Window, Help**. Settings and Quit are in
  **File**, About is in **Help**.

**File ▸ Save** means: open the **File** menu, click **Save**.

## Opening a show

1. Put the lesson's file in **Documents → qlcconsole** first. A browser
   saves to **Downloads**: move it.
2. **File ▸ Open**, pick the file, **Open**. Course shows end in `.qlcc`.

If it asks *"Do you wish to save the current workspace?"*, that's about
the show already open: **Yes** saves it, **No** drops its changes.

**File ▸ Open** greyed out: you're in Operate. Press **Stop**, then
**Design** at the top right.

## Saving

1. **Save As first, with your name:** **File ▸ Save As…** →
   `Lesson01-yourname.qlcc`. It opens in the lesson file's folder, and the
   lesson file stays as it came.
2. **Then ⌘S (Ctrl+S) as you work.** It saves over your own file.

How you can tell:

| Unsaved | Saved |
|---|---|
| The footer reads **● Unsaved changes**, in orange; the window title ends in **\*** | The footer reads **✓ Saved** |

**Autosaved** and a time in the footer is a recovery copy, not a save:
still save. To hand work in, take your `.qlcc` from **Documents →
qlcconsole** (USB stick, school drive or email).

## Undo

- **⌘Z** (Ctrl+Z) undoes the last change; **⇧⌘Z** (Ctrl+Shift+Z) redoes it.
- **Edit ▸ Undo** names the step first: *Undo …*.
- Each tab keeps its own undo: Build, Show Tree, Fixtures, Lighting Studio
  and Connections. Undo on the tab where you made the change. The Run tab
  has none.

## Help

**F1** opens the app's help on what you're looking at; **Help ▸ Getting
Started** is the overview. On a Mac laptop, F1 needs **fn**, or use the
**Help** menu.

## Quitting safely

1. Press **Stop** and click **Design**: the app won't quit in Operate.
2. Save (⌘S / Ctrl+S).
3. Quit: **⌘Q** on Mac; **Ctrl+Q** (or **File ▸ Quit qlcconsole**) on
   Windows.

Quit with unsaved changes and it asks *"Do you wish to save…?"*: **Yes**
saves, **No** drops the changes, **Cancel** goes back.
