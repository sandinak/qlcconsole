## The task

1. **Choose the clip.** Your teacher assigns one, or your group proposes
   one for approval.
2. **Design it on paper:** a cue sheet of 3–5 cues.

   | Cue | When | Feel | Lights (group: colour, level, aim) | Time (snap / fade / hold) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1.0.0 | | | | |
   | | | | | |

3. **Program it** from `lesson-1-1-start.qlcc` (the empty classroom show):
   **+ Production** named for your group, **+ Cue list**, then your cues
   with their layers, looks and times.
4. **Save** as `Level1-check-group.qlcc`: **File ▸ Save As** first, then
   **⌘S** (Ctrl+S) as you work.
5. **Run it live** while the clip plays: one of you calls as SM, another
   is on the board.
6. **Talk for one minute:** what you wanted the audience to feel, and how
   the lighting did it.

Everything you need is in Lessons 1.1–1.5: groups, colours and levels
(1.1), changing looks and cue looks (1.2), front light and aiming (1.3),
timing (1.4), calling and running (1.5).

## How it's marked

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Design** (the sheet) | Cues with no reasons | Some cues have a reason | Every cue has a reason that fits the music | Clear contrast and a story; colour, level, direction and timing all chosen on purpose |
| **Programming** | Fewer than 3 cues, or it doesn't play | Plays, but differs from the sheet | Plays what the sheet says | Plays the sheet, with faces lit and moving lights aimed |
| **Operation** | Cues missed or very late | Some cues early or late | Every cue on its call | Clean calls and GOs; a mistake recovered calmly (Back, LOAD) |
| **Teamwork** | One person did it | Roles shared unevenly | Everyone had a role | Everyone can explain every cue |

**Pass:** 3 or more in every row. Below 3 in a row, you get notes on what
to work on, then run it again.
