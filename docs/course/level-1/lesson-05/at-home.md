# At home — Lesson 1.5

Start the [Level 1 check](level-check.md). Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Pick the 30-second clip for the Level 1 check (or use the one your
   teacher gives you).
2. Write its **cue sheet**: 3–5 cues, with when, why, which lights, and
   the timing.
3. Download [`lesson-1-1-start.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-1-start.qlcc)
   (the empty classroom show) and start building it: **+ Production**,
   **+ Cue list**, your cues.
4. Run it from the **Run** tab while the clip plays. Ask someone at home
   to be your stage manager.
5. Save it as `Level1-check-yourname.qlcc` and bring it, with the cue
   sheet.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Pick the 30-second clip (or use the one your teacher gives you).
2. Write its **cue sheet**: 3–5 cues, with when, why, which lights (our
   groups, colours and levels), and the timing (snap, or a fade in
   seconds).
3. Practise **calling** it out loud while the clip plays: "Standby
   lights cue…", "GO". In class, your group builds it and you call it.
