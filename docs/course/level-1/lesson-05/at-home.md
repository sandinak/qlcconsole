# At home — Lesson 1.5

This week you start the [Level 1 check](level-check.md). Do **one** of
these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Choose the 30-second clip for the Level 1 check (or use the one your
   teacher assigns).
2. Write its **cue sheet**: 3–5 cues, each with when, why, which lights
   and the timing.
3. Download [`lesson-1-1-start.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-1-start.qlcc)
   (the empty classroom show) and start programming it: **+ Production**,
   **+ Cue list**, then your cues.
4. Run it from the **Run** tab while the clip plays, with someone at home
   calling it as your stage manager.
5. Save it as `Level1-check-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in with the cue
   sheet.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Choose the 30-second clip (or use the one your teacher assigns).
2. Write its **cue sheet**: 3–5 cues, each with when, why, which lights
   (our groups, colours and levels) and the timing (a snap, or a fade in
   seconds).
3. Practise **calling** it aloud while the clip plays ("Standby lights
   cue…", "GO"). In class, your group programs it and you call it.
