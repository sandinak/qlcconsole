## 1. Calling and running the show

- Title, Level 1 · Lesson 5

## 2. Who decides, who presses

- **Stage manager**: watches, calls. **Board op**: presses GO.
- *Say:* "The SM decides when; the op makes it happen."

## 3. The call

- "Standby lights cue one-oh-two." — "Standing by." — "Lights cue
  one-oh-two… GO."
- *Say:* "Only on the word GO."

## 4. The calling drill

- Pairs: call, clap on GO. Try to catch each other out.

## 5. The Run tab

- Picture: `images/lesson-1-5/run-standby.png` (numbers 1–6).
- GO (Space) · Back · Stop · NEXT · STANDBY.

## 6. Operate: LIVE

- **Operate** (top right): only GO changes the stage.
- **Design**: back to building.

## 7. LOAD and jump

- Click a cue: **STANDBY** — the next GO plays it.
- Double-click: jump there now.

## 8. Your turn

- SM · Board op · Spotter — switch every run.
- Handout Part 2, then the song.
