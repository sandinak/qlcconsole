## 2. Where does the light change?

- Play a 60-second clip of a show choir opener.
- *Ask:* "Where did the feeling change?" — collect answers.

## 3. A cue is a moment

- **Cue** = a moment where the light changes.
- **Cue list** = all of a song's cues, in order.
- **Production** = the whole show.

## 4. Every cue has a reason

| Moment | Feel | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Pose | mystery, waiting | coloured cyc, singers in silhouette |
| Verse | close, personal | warm light on the singers |
| Chorus | big, joyful | strong colour on the risers |

- *Say:* "We never add light just because we can."

## 6. Groups, layers and looks

- **Group** — lights we use together: *TrussC-RGB*.
- **Layer** — one group doing one thing in a cue.
- **Look** — its colour and its intensity: *Blue, Full*.
- **Palette** — a saved colour or level you drag on.
