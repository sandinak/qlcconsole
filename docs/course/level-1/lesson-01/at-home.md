# At home — Lesson 1.1

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole (see [Install](../../install.md)).
2. Download [`lesson-1-1-start.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-1-start.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**).
3. Build Pose, Verse and Chorus again **without the handout pictures**,
   going back to the handout's steps only if you need them.
4. Then make the design **your own**: recolour it to tell a different
   story, such as a ballad or a horror number, changing at least one
   colour in every cue.
5. Play it back from the **Run** tab (GO three times).
6. Save it as `Lesson01-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in (on a USB
   stick or in the class folder), or hand in a screenshot of each cue in
   the Rig Overview.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Choose a song (school-appropriate) and listen closely to its first
   minute.
2. Write a cue sheet for it with **three to five cues**:

   | Cue | When (words or time) | What should the audience feel? | Lights (group: colour, level) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1.0.0 | | | |
   | 1.0.1 | | | |
   | 1.0.2 | | | |

   Use our groups (Steps, or US, CS and DS on their own; StepTops,
   TrussC-RGB, TrussW-RGB, Truss-Spots, USTs, AT-W-ALL, AT-Spots,
   Lanterns), our colours (White, Warm White, Red, Amber, Yellow, Green,
   Cyan, Blue, Lavender, Magenta) and our levels (Full, 75%, Half, 25%,
   10%, Out).
3. Next class, a partner programs your cue sheet in the Show Tree while
   you check that it matches your design.
