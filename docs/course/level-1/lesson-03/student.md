words:
  - term: Front / back / side light
    means: "From the audience side (shows faces) / from behind (outlines performers) / from the side (sculpts bodies)."
  - term: Uplight
    means: "Light from below. Our step tops uplight the choir."
  - term: Moving light
    means: "A fixture you can point remotely: it **pans** (left–right) and **tilts** (up–down)."
  - term: Position
    means: "Where a moving light points. Without one, it points wherever it was left."
  - term: Stage target
    means: "A named spot to aim at: **Center** (the middle of the CS row) and **Soloist DS** (downstage centre)."
  - term: Spot
    means: "A narrow, hard-edged light for one performer, like our **AT-Spots**."
  - term: Shutter
    means: "Blocks a spot's beam. Ours opens by itself when the spot gets a level."
  - term: Block cue
    means: "A cue that starts from a dark stage: nothing tracks into it."

## Why

Visibility comes first: an audience that can't see faces can't follow
the song ([Lesson 0.1](../0-1/)). Front light is the part of a look that
shows them ([Lesson 0.3](../0-3/)), and a special picks one singer out
of the choir ([Lesson 0.4](../0-4/)).

## The plan

| Cue | When | Why | Lights |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0.1 Verse *(add)* | First words | | **AT-W-ALL**: Warm White, Full, aimed at **Center** |
| 1.0.4 Solo *(new)* | After the scream | | **Block**. **AT-Spots**: White, Full, aimed at **Soloist DS** |

In the solo, where will the audience look, and why?

## Steps

1. **File ▸ Open** → `lesson-1-3-start.qlcc`.
2. Click the **Show Tree** tab (⇧⌘5); open **View ▸ Rig Overview** (⇧⌘R)
   beside it.
3. Click **1.0.1 Verse**. Drag **AT-W-ALL** (Groups → AT) onto the dashed
   box under **Layers**.
4. Drag **Warm White**, then **Full**, onto the **AT-W-ALL** row. Light
   spills onto the floor and the house: the washes aren't aimed yet.

   ![Front light, not aimed](../../images/lesson-1-3/rig-verse-front.png)

5. Open **All → Targets** and drag **Center** onto the **AT-W-ALL** row.
   The beams swing onto the risers.

   ![Front light, aimed at Center](../../images/lesson-1-3/rig-verse-center.png)

6. Click **1.0.3 Scream**, then **+ Cue**, type `Solo`, press **Return**.
7. On the Solo's page, set **Mode** to **Block**. The grey rows go and
   the stage goes dark.
8. Drag **AT-Spots** (Groups → AT) onto the dashed box.
9. Drag **White** and **Full** onto the **AT-Spots** row. Two pools of
   light land at the foot of the towers: no position yet.
10. Drag **Soloist DS** (All → Targets) onto the **AT-Spots** row. Both
    spots swing onto the singer in front of the risers.

    ![The solo](../../images/lesson-1-3/rig-solo.png)

11. Click the **AT-Spots** row: a spot needs a level and a position (plus
    a colour).

    ![What a spot needs](../../images/lesson-1-3/spot-layer.png)

12. **File ▸ Save As** → `Lesson1-3-yourname.qlcc`.

**Stuck?** No targets: type `Center` in **Filter the Library**. The Solo
is still red: set **Mode** to **Block**. Spots hard to see: in the Rig
Overview set **Room** to **Dim**.

## Exit ticket

1. Which of our lights light the singers' **faces**, and from which
   direction?
2. Why does a **moving light** need a position?
3. Our spots' shutter rests closed. Why did they light at White and Full
   without a Shutter look?
4. Why is the Solo a **Block** cue?
