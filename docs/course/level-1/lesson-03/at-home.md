# At home — Lesson 1.3

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole if you haven't yet (see [Install](../../install.md)).
2. Download [`lesson-1-3-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-3-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**).
3. Add a cue after the Solo, **1.0.5 Duet**, for two singers at
   **Center**. Make it a **Block** cue and light it with the **AT-W-ALL**
   washes, aimed at **Center**, in a colour and level of your choice.
   (The washes have no shutter, so they don't need a shutter look.)
4. Add **back light** or **uplight** to the duet. Which direction makes it
   most dramatic?
5. Save it as `Lesson1-3-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in (on a USB
   stick or in the class folder), or hand in a screenshot of the duet in
   the Rig Overview.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Draw a plan of our stage, like the Level 0 stage plan.
2. Mark a soloist downstage centre, and draw an arrow for each light you
   would use on her solo, from where it hangs to where it lands. Label
   each arrow with its direction (front, back, side or below) and its
   colour.
3. In a sentence or two, explain why you chose those directions.
