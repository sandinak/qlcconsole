# At home — Lesson 1.3

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole if you haven't: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Download [`lesson-1-3-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-3-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub: open it, then **Download raw file**).
3. Add a cue after the Solo, **1.0.5 Duet**: two singers at **Center**.
   Make it **Block**, then light it with the **AT-W-ALL** washes in a
   colour and a level you choose, aimed at **Center**. (The washes light up without
   a shutter look.)
4. Add some **back light** or **uplight** to the duet. Which direction
   makes it feel the most dramatic?
5. Save it as `Lesson1-3-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it (USB stick or the
   class folder), or hand in a screenshot of the duet in the Rig Overview.

Nothing you do can harm real lights: the course show isn't connected to
any.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Draw our stage from above (like the Level 0 stage plan).
2. Draw a soloist downstage centre. Draw arrows for the light you would
   use for her solo: where each light comes **from** and where it goes
   **to**. Label each arrow: front, back, side or below, and its colour.
3. Write one sentence: why did you choose those directions?
