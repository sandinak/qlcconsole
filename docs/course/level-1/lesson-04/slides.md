## 1. Timing: fades and holds

- Title, Level 1 · Lesson 4
- Picture: `images/lesson-1-4/run-fading.png`
- *Say:* "Light moves with the music."

## 2. A snap or a fade?

- The room lights: off at once; then slowly.
- *Ask:* "Which was a surprise? Which was a breath?"

## 3. Follow the music

- Chorus on a hit → snap. Verse easing in → a 2–3 s fade. The start → a
  slow fade up.

## 4. Count it

- Play the song; count each change on your fingers.

## 5. On the cue's page

- Picture: `images/lesson-1-4/cue-timing.png`
- **Fade in** · **Fade out** · **Hold** (∞ waits for GO).

## 6. Fades play on GO

- Clicking a cue shows it at once; **GO** plays the fade.

## 7. A hold that follows on

- The Scream: hold 2 s → the Solo plays by itself; fade out 3 s → the red
  melts away.
