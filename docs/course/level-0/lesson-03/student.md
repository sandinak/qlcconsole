## Why

No single light does every job. Front light shows faces but flattens
bodies; back light makes shapes but hides faces. A look is built in
parts, each doing one job. In the app, each part is a **layer**.

## The parts of a look

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Front light + fill | Faces: visibility |
| Back light | Lifts singers off the background |
| Side light | Shape and movement: the dance break |
| Background (cyc) | Mood, time and place |
| Wash | One colour over an area |
| Special | Focus: one person or place |

Build in this order: **faces first**, then separation, then background
and colour, then specials.

1. Take this look apart. For each part: where from, what colour, which

## Exit ticket

1. Which part of a look do you build **first**?
2. What is the difference between a **wash** and a **special**?
3. The back row disappears into the cyc. Which part would you add?

## At home

Do one:

- **Photo:** take a photo of a live performance apart: each part, its
  colour, its job.
- **Torch:** in a dim room, light a face from the front, side, behind
  and below. One line each: how it looks, what song it suits.
