# Lesson 0.3 — Building a look

**Student handout**

## Why this matters

No single light does every job. Light from the front shows faces but
flattens bodies against the cyc; light from behind makes shapes but
hides faces. A designer builds a **look** in parts, each part doing one
job, so the whole thing reads. When you program, each part becomes a
**layer**.

## Today you will

- learn the parts of a look and the job of each;
- take a look on our stage apart, part by part;
- design a look on paper for a ballad verse.

## Words for today

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Front light (key)** | The main light on the performers' faces, from in front and a little above. Ours comes from the **audience towers**. |
| **Fill** | Softer light from the other side, so the key light's shadows aren't black. |
| **Back light** | Light from behind and above. It outlines heads and shoulders and lifts the singers off the background. |
| **Side light** | Light from the side. It shapes bodies and shows movement: the dancer's light. |
| **Uplight** | Light from below: dramatic or eerie. Our risers' step lights uplight the choir. |
| **Background** | What is behind the performers. Ours is the **cyc**, lit in colour. |
| **Wash** | A soft, wide light that covers an area evenly. |
| **Special** | A light for one place or one person: a soloist's spot. |
| **Layer** | One part of a look: one set of lights doing one job. |

## The parts of a look

| Part | Job | In a show choir number |
|---|---|---|
| Front light + fill | Visibility: faces | Every row lit, both sides of every face |
| Back light | Separation: singers stand out from the background | Rims of light on the back row's heads |
| Side light | Shape and movement | The dance break, bodies in motion |
| Background (cyc) | Mood, time and place: most of the colour on stage | Blue for night, red for danger |
| Wash | Covers an area in one colour | The whole choir in warm amber |
| Special | Focus: one person or place | The soloist downstage centre |

Build in this order: **faces first**, then separation, then the
background and colour, then the specials. A look with great colour and no
faces has failed.
