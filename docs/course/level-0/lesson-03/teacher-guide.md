# Lesson 0.3 — Building a look

**Teacher guide** · Level 0, lesson 3 · 25 minutes · no computers ·
pairs with Lesson 0.4 in one period

**Give students:** <https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-3/> (the lesson's ideas, the activity, the exit ticket and the at-home task; nothing else)

<img src="../../images/qr/class-0-3.svg" width="120" alt="QR code for https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-3/">

## Why this lesson

Students tend to think of "the lights" as one thing. A look is layers,
each doing one job, and the Show Tree is built the same way: a cue holds
**layers**, one group doing one thing. Learning to take a look apart
here makes Lesson 1.1's layers obvious.

## Objectives

Students can:

1. name the parts of a look (front light and fill, back light, side light,
   uplight, background, wash, special) and the job of each;
2. take a look apart into its parts;
3. design a look in layers, faces first.

## Vocabulary (introduce in this order)

**front light (key)**, **fill**, **back light**, **side light**,
**uplight**, **background**, **wash**, **special**, **layer**. Each is in
the handout and the [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## 1. The parts (8 min)

With the torch: front (*"you can see the face, but it's flat"*), add a
second light from the other side (*"fill: the shadows soften"*), side
(*"now the face has shape"*), behind (*"a rim of light, the head stands
out from the wall"*), below (*"horror film"*). Then the table on the
handout.

**Key idea to land:** each part does one job. Build faces first; colour
and effects come after.

## 2. Take a look apart (7 min)

Project `rig-verse-center.png`. Pairs list its parts. Then ask what's
missing (*back light; nothing separates the back row from the cyc*) and
what they'd add.

## 3. Design a look (7 min)

The ballad verse on the handout, in layers. Push for a reason in the Job
column of every row. Ask one pair to read theirs out; the class checks:
*are the faces lit? Does the soloist stand out?*

## 4. Exit ticket (3 min)

[Exit ticket](exit-ticket.md) — answers below.

## Extension

Compare `rig-verse-center.png` with `rig-solo.png`: which parts were
taken away for the solo, and why does the soloist read more strongly
with less light on stage?

## At home

[At home](at-home.md): take a look apart from a performance photo, or
light a face with a torch from four directions.

## Answer key

- **Take a look apart** (`rig-verse-center`): the cyc in blue from the
  trusses (background: mood); warm white front light from both audience
  towers (visibility, with each tower filling the other's shadows);
  low lavender uplight from the step tops on the risers (mood). Missing:
  back light, so the back row sits flat against the cyc; no special, so
  nobody is picked out.
- **Design a look:** any set of layers where faces are lit (a low front
  wash on the choir, a brighter special on the soloist), the soloist is
  the brightest thing, and the colours fit "quiet, lonely" (cool or pale
  background, low levels), each with a job.
- **Exit ticket:** 1 — front light (key, with fill). 2 — a wash covers an
  area evenly; a special lights one person or place. 3 — back light: it
  outlines the singers and separates them from the background.
- **Extension:** the cyc and front wash went out; only the soloist's
  specials remain, so the eye has nowhere else to go (contrast, Lesson
  0.4).
