## Why

A number tells its story in sections. If the light looks the same all
the way through, the audience loses the song's shape; if it changes for
no reason, they lose the singers. Know the song first, then choose
lights.

## How a designer works

1. **Listen**, several times, with the lyrics.
2. **What is it about?** One sentence.
3. **Feel each section.** What should the audience feel?
4. **Watch the staging.** Where are the singers? Who has a feature? When
   does the formation change? The light must be where they will be.
5. **Then the lights.**

## Rules of thumb

- Each section looks different from its neighbours.
- One look per idea: change when the song changes.
- Verse close and small; chorus open; bridge somewhere new.
- Save something for the final chorus.

## Exit ticket

1. Name **four** sections of a song, in the order they usually come.
2. Why should the chorus look different from the verse?
3. Name **two** things a designer does before choosing any lights.

## At home

Do one:

- **Song map:** a song you like. Listen twice; sections, starts, feel and
  a look idea for each.
- **Paper:** the sections of a song you know by heart, one word for the
  feel of each. Mark the biggest and the smallest.
