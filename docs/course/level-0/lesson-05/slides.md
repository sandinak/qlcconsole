## 3. The sections

- Intro · verse · pre-chorus · chorus · bridge · dance break · final
  chorus · button.

## 4. How a designer works

- Listen → what is it about → feel each section → watch the staging →
  then the lights.

## 5. Light the staging

- *Say:* "The light has to be where the singers will be. A formation
  change is often the best moment for a cue."

## 6. Rules of thumb

- Each section different from its neighbours. One look per idea. Verse
  close, chorus open, bridge new, save something for the end.
