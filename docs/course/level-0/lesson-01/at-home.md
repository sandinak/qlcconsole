# At home — Lesson 0.1

Do **one**.

## A. Light doing a job (anywhere)

Light is designed outside the theatre too: a shop window, a restaurant,
a game's half-time show, a concert stream, a church service. Find
**two** places and log them:

## B. On paper

Pick a song you know well. Write one sentence for each of three jobs:
what would the light do for this song? *Example:* "Mood: a breakup song,
so cool and dim in the verses."
