# At home — Lesson 0.6

Do **one**.

## A. Round 2 of your own song map

Take the song map you made at home after Lesson 0.5 (or start one). Mark
each build (↗), each hit (★), snap or fade (S / F) at every change, the
peak and the button.

## B. Pace a show on paper

Write a five-song set list for a show choir show (real songs or made-up
titles: opener, ballad, up-tempo, feature, closer, in any order you
like). Give each an energy level from 1 to 5 for its lighting, and say in
one line where the show peaks and why.
