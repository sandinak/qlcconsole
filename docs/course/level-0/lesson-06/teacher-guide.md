# Lesson 0.6 — Anticipation and momentum

**Teacher guide** · Level 0, lesson 6 · 25–30 minutes · no computers ·
pairs with Lesson 0.5 in one period · **Level 0 check, part 1**

**Give students:** <https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-6/> (the lesson's ideas, the activity, the exit ticket and the at-home task; nothing else)

<img src="../../images/qr/class-0-6.svg" width="120" alt="QR code for https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-6/">

## Why this lesson

New designers light every moment at full. This lesson is about time:
what comes before a big moment, what comes after, and what to save. It
is the reason behind Lesson 1.4's fades and holds and Level 2's hits
and sub-cues.

## Objectives

Students can:

1. explain how a build, holding back and a hit make a moment feel bigger;
2. choose snap or fade for a change and say why;
3. mark a song's builds, hits, peak and button, and pace a set list so
   the show doesn't peak in song one.

## Vocabulary (introduce in this order)

## 1. Snap or fade (5 min)

With the room's light switch (or a dimmer if you have one): count "5, 6,
7, 8" and snap the room lights off on "1". Then do it as a slow fade
over the count, if you can. *"Which one was the end of a song? Which was
a sunset?"* Then: snap it a beat late. *"How did that feel?"* (Wrong.)
**Key idea to land:** timing is part of the look.

## 2. How light builds momentum (7 min)

The list on the handout. Use the pictures as a sequence: the pose (one
colour, the singers dark: holding back), the chorus (the risers open
up), the scream (everything red: the hit). *"If the first chorus had been
the scream, where would the song have to go next?"*

## 3. Song map, round 2 (10 min)

Play the song again from the start. Students mark ↗, ★, S/F, PEAK and
the button on their 0.5 map. Pause after the first chorus to check one
together. Push for **holding back**: what are they saving for the final
chorus?

## 4. Pacing a show (4 min)

The set list on the handout. Take two answers. Land: the closer is the
peak; the ballad is a rest; the opener is big but not the biggest.

## 5. Exit ticket and Level 0 check, part 1 (4 min)

[Exit ticket](exit-ticket.md), then collect the song maps.

**Level 0 check, part 1: the song map.** A map passes when it has:

- every section of the song, in order, with a start;
- a light change at each section, each with **what the audience should
  feel**;
- at least one build and one hit marked, S or F at every change;
- a peak, and a button.

Return maps that miss two or more of these with a note; they redo them
at home. Part 2 (label the stage) is in Lesson 0.8.

## Extension

*"Where would you put a blackout in this song, other than the end? What
would it say?"* (A sudden stop before the final chorus; a shock.)

## At home

[At home](at-home.md): a song map round 2 for their at-home song from
0.5, or a set list paced on paper.

## Answer key

- **Exit ticket:** 1 — the last note: snap (a button); the start of a
  ballad's verse: fade. 2 — so the final chorus can be bigger; if chorus
  1 uses everything, the song has nowhere to go. 3 — the final hit that
  ends a number, usually a snap to a big look or to black on the last
  note; it tells the audience to applaud.
- **Pacing:** opener 4, ballad 2, up-tempo 3–4, feature solo 2–3, closer
  5. Any pacing that rests after the opener and peaks at the closer.
