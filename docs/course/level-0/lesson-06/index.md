# Lesson 0.6 — Anticipation and momentum

No computers; the song from Lesson 0.5 again. How light makes an
audience lean forward: builds, holding back, hits on the accents, a snap
or a fade, the button, and pacing a whole show. Students finish their
song map and hand it in as part 1 of the Level 0 check. About 25–30
minutes; pairs with Lesson 0.5.
