# At home — Lesson 0.4

Do **one**.

## A. One performer picked out

In a concert, music video or stage clip, find a moment where the light
picks out one performer. Write:

1. what you watched, and when;
2. how the light did it (brighter on them? darker everywhere else? a
   different colour? movement?);
3. what it made you feel about that performer.
