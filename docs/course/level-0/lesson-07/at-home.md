# At home — Lesson 0.7

Do **one**.

## A. Call a song

Play a song for someone at home. You are the SM; they flip a light
switch (or hold up a hand) on each GO. Call three changes: standby
first, then GO on the moment. Then swap. Write one line on what was
hardest.

## B. Write the calls

On your song map from Lessons 0.5–0.6, number each change as a cue and
write where the **standby** goes (about 10–15 seconds before) and the
exact word or beat for each **GO**. Then mark one moment where you would
keep a hit ready for busking, and say why.
