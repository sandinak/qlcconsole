# At home — Lesson 0.2

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md). Make a folder
   **Documents → qlcconsole** for your shows.
2. Download [`lesson-1-1-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-1-finish.qlcc)
   (on GitHub: open it, then **Download raw file**) into that folder.
3. **File → Open** it, then **View → Rig Overview**.
4. Take a **screenshot** of the stage from an angle you like, with
   **Room: Work light**, and label five parts on it (any drawing app, or
   print it and write on it).
5. Play the three cues in the **Run** tab. Which one do you like best,
   and why?

Nothing you do can harm real lights: the course show isn't connected to
any.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Teach someone at home the stage directions: stand facing them as the
   "audience" and call "stage left!", "upstage!". Then swap.
2. Draw the stage of a place you know (a school stage, a church, a
   concert you saw) from above. Label upstage, downstage, stage left and
   stage right, and where the lights were.
