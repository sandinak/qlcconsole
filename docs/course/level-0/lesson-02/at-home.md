# At home — Lesson 0.2

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole (see [Install](../../install.md)) and make a folder
   **Documents → qlcconsole** for your shows.
2. Download [`lesson-1-1-finish.qlcc`](../../shows/lesson-1-1-finish.qlcc)
   into that folder (on GitHub, open it and click **Download raw file**).
3. Open it with **File → Open**, then choose **View → Rig Overview**.
4. With **Room: Work light**, take a **screenshot** of the stage from an
   angle you like and label five parts on it, in any drawing app or on a
   printout.
5. Play the three cues in the **Run** tab. Which one works best for you,
   and why?

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Teach the stage directions to someone at home: stand facing them as
   your "audience", call "stage left!" or "upstage!", and check they
   move the right way. Then swap.
2. Draw a plan (a view from above) of a stage you know: a school stage, a
   church, a concert venue. Label upstage, downstage, stage left and stage
   right, and mark where the lights were.
