# Lesson 0.2 — What light does

**Teacher guide** · Level 0, lesson 2 · 25 minutes · no computers ·
pairs with Lesson 0.1 in one period

**Give students:** <https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-2/> (the lesson's ideas, the activity, the exit ticket and the at-home task; nothing else)

<img src="../../images/qr/class-0-2.svg" width="120" alt="QR code for https://sandinak.github.io/qlcconsole/class/0-2/">

## Why this lesson

0.1 gave the jobs; this gives the controls. The four qualities are the
vocabulary every later lesson uses, in the app too: the Show Tree's rows
are intensity, colour and position, and its timing is movement.

## Objectives

Students can:

1. name the four qualities of light: intensity, colour, direction,
   movement;
2. describe a light change by its quality and its job ("cooler and dimmer
   for a calm verse");
3. say which direction lights faces.

## Vocabulary (introduce in this order)

**intensity** (level), **colour** (warm / cool, saturated / pale),
**direction** (front, back, side, below), **movement**, **fade**,
**snap**. Each is in the handout and the [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## 1. The four qualities (8 min)

Use [slides](slides.md) 2–6. Each pair of pictures is the same lights with
one quality changed:

| Quality | Ask | Pictures |
|---|---|---|
| **Intensity** | "Which is the big moment?" | `intensity-full`, `intensity-low` |
| **Colour** | "Which is the love song? Which is night?" | `colour-warm`, `colour-cool` |
| **Direction** | "Which shows faces? Which hides them?" | `direction-front`, `direction-back`, `direction-below` |
| **Movement** | "What moved in the clip?" | (the clip) |

**Key idea to land:** every change is a change to at least one quality.
"Make it different" isn't a note; "make it cooler and dimmer" is.

## 2. Light log (10 min)

Play the clip again. Students watch only the light and fill in the log:
*when*, *which quality*, *which job* (from 0.1). Pause if they fall behind.

## 3. Share and sort (4 min)

- One change per group, in the form "at ___, the ___ changed, to ___".
- Picture sort (cards): sort by quality, then pick one for a sad ballad
  and one for a big opener, and say why.

## 4. Exit ticket (3 min)

[Exit ticket](exit-ticket.md) — answers below.

## Extension

The handout's last box: one moment of "Everybody Scream", a quality and a
job.

## At home

[At home](at-home.md): log three changes in any performance, or match the
pictures to songs on paper.

## Answer key

- **Exit ticket:** 1 — intensity, colour, direction, movement. 2 — front
  light. 3 — any change named by quality and job, e.g. "it went dim and
  blue in the verse (intensity, colour) to make it calm (mood)".
- **Picture sort:** intensity — `intensity-full`, `intensity-low`; colour —
  `colour-warm`, `colour-cool`; direction — `direction-front`,
  `direction-back`, `direction-below`.
