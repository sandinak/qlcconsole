# At home — Lesson 3.8

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-8-yourname.qlcc`. Add a third song as a **+ Section**, named for a song you know, with two or three cues, and make it Block. Make a second override hit, **Quiet**, that pulls the cyc to 25%. Save it as `Lesson3-8-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Write the night's checklist for a show you have worked or seen, with the person who does each item: the preshow, the first GO, the intermission, the strike.
