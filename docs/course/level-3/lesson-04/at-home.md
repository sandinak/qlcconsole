# At home — Lesson 3.4

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-4-yourname.qlcc` (or do the lesson's steps again on
   [`ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`](../../shows/ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc)).
3. Add a second par on **SR Boom** and point it at the soloist's mark: set
   its **Face**, then **Points** and **Tilt in yoke**. Use **Show Light
   Footprint** to check where it lands.
4. Save it as `Lesson3-4-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it (USB stick or the
   class folder), or hand in a screenshot of the plot.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Measure a room you know: the ceiling height and the width of one wall.
2. Draw it from above as a plot with a boom in one corner and a door in a
   wall, and mark where a light on the boom would point.
3. Write the light's Face, Points and Tilt in yoke.
