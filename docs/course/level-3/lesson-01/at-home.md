# At home — Lesson 3.1

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-1-yourname.qlcc` (or do the lesson's steps again on
   [`ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`](../../shows/ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc)).
3. Add four **WLED ▸ Lantern** lights in Effect (15ch) mode with **Quantity**
   4 and an **Address gap** of 5. Read the four addresses the app chose.
4. Open **Fixtures → Patch List…**, group by **Universe**, and save the list
   as `Lesson3-1-Home-yourname.xlsx`. Bring it (USB stick or the class
   folder), or hand in a screenshot.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Patch a small rig on a printed 512 grid: six LED pars at 7 channels,
   two movers at 16 channels and a dimmer pack of four channels. Write each
   light's start and end, and the label you'd put on the first par's cable end (D1.1).
2. Write the label you'd put on the second mover's cable end.
