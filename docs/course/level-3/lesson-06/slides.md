# Slides — Lesson 3.6: Focus and calibration

*An outline: one heading per slide, with what's on it and what the
teacher says. It can be turned into a deck as it is.*

---

## 1. Focus and calibration

- Title, Level 3 · Lesson 6
- Picture: a designer on stage, back to a single beam
- *Say:* "The design is written once. Every room is different."

## 2. Who stands where

- **Electrician at the light** (ladder, lift, or the controller).
- **Designer at the point**, back to the light, reading the shadow.
- Board op and crew on headset; the laptop's voice in the headsets.

## 3. The words

- "Unit 3." · "Hot spot on me." · "Up a little." · "Stage left."
- "Spot down" / "Flood out": the size. "Sharper" / "Softer": the edge.
- "Lock it." → "Locked." On our rig: **A**.
- **Home**: pan and tilt at the middle; a lost mover is sent home.

## 4. Where is it? The stage marks

- **Curtain line**: where the main curtain hangs.
- **Number line**: feet out from **CL**, SR one way, SL the other.
- **Apron**: the deck in front of the curtain line.
- On the plot's **Stage** layer (Plot layers), each with its own eye.
- Picture: the classroom plot with the marks. "SL 1 · DS 1."

## 5. Venue focus: the focus calls

- A call is some lights and their point: **Truss-Spots → Center**.
- The show keeps the calls in order; every room is focused the same way.
- Lighting Studio ▸ **Focus ▸ Edit focus calls…**, then **Start venue focus**.

## 6. The venue keeps the corrections

- Each A writes a file beside the show: `Lesson3-6-you.venues.xml`.
- The footer says which room: **Venue: Classroom**.
- The show file never changes.
- Touring: the **tour package** travels; each room's **house rig**,
  trims, marks and outputs live in its venue.

## 7. The controller

- RT / LT calls · RB / LB lights · stick nudges · **A** lock · **B** back
  · **Y** the whole call · D-pad: spot down, flood out, sharper, softer.
- Keys: PgDn / PgUp · ] / [ · arrows · Return · Backspace · G · , . ' ;

## 8. The scramble

- Every mover knocked off its target by a random amount (Easy: 1–3°).
  Focus them back.
- Practice venue: your real venues are never touched.
- Score: within 1°, average, worst, saves, time.
- **Hand-in: the focus report (PDF).**

## 9. Focus this cue: the cue, not the rig

- Same controller, on a cue: spot down, keep in the cue.
- An aimed layer follows its target; one spot can be an exception.
- *Ask:* "Which one goes to the next room with the show?"
