# At home — Lesson 3.6

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-6-yourname.qlcc` (or do the lesson's Get ready
   steps again on
   [`ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`](../../shows/ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc),
   saved under your own name first).
3. **Lighting Studio → Focus → Practice (scramble)…** on **Medium:
   2–6° off**. Focus every light back and end the calls.
4. **Export the report…** as a PDF and bring it, with your Easy report
   from class. Under the score, write two lines: which call took
   longest, and why.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Draw a room at home from above: mark a **curtain line** (a doorway
   will do), a **centre line**, and a **number line** across the front in
   feet.
2. Put three "targets" in it (a chair, a lamp, a doorway) and write each
   one's place the way the plot does: "SL 3' · US 4'".
3. Write a focus chart for two lights aimed at them: the light, where it
   hangs, its point, and the words you would call to focus it.
