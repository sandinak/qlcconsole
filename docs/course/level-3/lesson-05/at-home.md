# At home — Lesson 3.5

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-5-yourname.qlcc`. The sound op has a slower recording: every time is 10 seconds later after the Verse. Change them, and read the Run tab to check the order. Save it as `Lesson3-5-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it in.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. For a song you know, write eight cues as time · number · what happens. Mark the two you would not put on the clock, and say why.
