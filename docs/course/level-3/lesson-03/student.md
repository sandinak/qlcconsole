## On paper

On the printed plot, find one of each, sketch its symbol and count them:
a moving light, a wash, a spot (hard edge), a practical.

Roles: Driver, Navigator, Designer (compares the plot with our rig).
Switch after each correction.

## Steps

1. **File → Open** → `ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`.
2. Click the **Lighting Studio** tab (⇧⌘3): the stage from above.
3. On the plot's bar, click **Show** and tick **Show Auto fixtures as
   their closest plot symbol**.
4. With the Designer, write down two guesses that are wrong for our rig.
5. Click the **Fixtures** tab (⇧⌘2). Right-click the group **AT-W-ALL**
   (folder **AT**) → **Plot Symbol ▸**. The first item, **Auto (Moving
   head spot)**, is the app's guess.
6. Choose **Moving head round wash**. Back in the **Lighting Studio**, the
   four tower washes have a round base and lens.
7. In **Fixtures**, right-click the **Lanterns** group → **Plot Symbol ▸**
   → under **Practicals**, **Lantern**. The six lanterns are drawn as
   lanterns.
8. Click the **Show Tree** tab (⇧⌘5) and open the Library's groups: the
   same icons. A symbol is only the picture; the lights send what they
   did before.
9. **File → Save As** → `Lesson3-3-yourname.qlcc`.

**Stuck?** Wrong symbol: **⌘Z**. Only one lantern changed: right-click
the **group**, not one lantern.

## Exit ticket

1. What is a **practical**? Give an example that isn't a lantern.
2. You set the AT-W-ALL washes to *Moving head round wash*. Did that
   change what they send to the lights? Why or why not?
3. What does **Auto** mean for a light's plot symbol?
4. Who reads a plot, and why does it matter that each symbol is right?
