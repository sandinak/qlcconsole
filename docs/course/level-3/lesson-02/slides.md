## 1. Fixtures and definitions

- Title, Level 3 · Lesson 2
- Picture: a manual's DMX chart
- *Say:* "The console doesn't know lights. It knows definitions."

## 2. A definition

- Its channels, what each does, the modes, the heads, its size and beam.
- A file, one per model.

## 3. Modes

- The same light, several footprints: 3ch, 5ch, 8ch.
- The mode on the light and on the console must match.

## 4. Heads

- A pixel box: 45 colour pixels and 30 white pixels, 75 heads.
- Groups: TrussC-RGB (colour heads) and TrussC-W (white heads).

## 5. In qlcconsole

- Fixtures tab: Manufacturer, Model, Mode, Type, Definition.
- Right-click → Definition & Mode…; Edit Fixture Definition….
- File → Show Definitions…: what the show carries.
