# At home — Lesson 3.2

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-2-yourname.qlcc` (or do the lesson's steps again on
   [`ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`](../../shows/ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc)).
3. Open **Fixtures → Fixture Editor…** and look up a light of a make you know
   (**File → Open**). Write down its modes, each mode's channels and heads, and
   the channel that dims it.
4. Bring the list (USB stick or the class folder), or hand in a screenshot
   of the **Modes** tab. Don't save the definition.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Find the DMX chart for a light (the manual, or the maker's website).
2. Copy it as a table: mode, channel number, what it does. Mark the channel
   that dims it and the ones that set its colour.
3. Work out how many of that light fit in one universe in each mode.
