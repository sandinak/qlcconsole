# Lesson 3.7 — House lights, work lights and power

Not every circuit on the console is a show light. Students add the house
lights, the work lights and a power relay to the classroom show, give each
the **output rules** it needs, and work out what Blackout, the Grand
Master and Blind should and shouldn't touch.
