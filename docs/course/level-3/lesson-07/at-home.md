# At home — Lesson 3.7

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open your `Lesson3-7-yourname.qlcc` (or do the lesson's steps again on
   [`ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc`](../../shows/ShowChoir-Classroom.qlcc)).
3. Add an intermission to the preshow list: a cue that brings the house
   back to full and the stage to a quiet look, and one that takes the
   house out again for Act 2.
4. Save it as `Lesson3-7-Home-yourname.qlcc` and bring it (USB stick or the
   class folder), or hand in a screenshot of the cue list.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Fill in the show-day table from Part 1 of the handout for a show you
   have been to or worked on.
2. Write the preshow and intermission sequence as cues: number, name,
   what the house and the stage do, and the fade time.
