## The task

1. **Open the show** and run **Review**. Read the **Not ready** list in the
   footer. Fix what is wrong, or **Not a Problem…** it with a note. An open blocker
   fails the check.
2. **The preshow:** Stage Power and the house lights as in
   [Lesson 3.7](../3-7/); the focus call for the song's spots
   ([Lesson 3.6](../3-6/)); every group master at full; the pad or the phone
   page set as the plan says ([Lesson 3.5](../3-5/)).
3. **Run one song.** One of you calls as SM, one is the op, one is at the
   stage. Fire a hit once. When a light is dark that shouldn't be, ask it
   why with **Why is this on?**.
4. **Talk for two minutes:** what you checked before the house opened, and
   why in that order.

Everything you need is in Lessons 3.1–3.9, on top of Level 2.

## How it's marked

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Checks** | Skipped the Review or Not ready | Read them; fixed some | Every blocker and warning fixed or marked Not a Problem with a note | Notes that would help the next person; fixed the cheap things first |
| **Preshow** | Items missed | Done out of order | Complete and in order | Complete, in order, and each item said aloud |
| **Operation** | Cues missed or very late | Some early or late | Every cue on its call; the hit on the music | Clean calls; a fault found and fixed calmly |
| **Teamwork** | One person did it | Roles shared unevenly | Everyone had a role | Everyone can explain every step and why |

**Pass:** 3 or more in every row. Below 3 in a row, you get notes on what to
work on, then run it again.
