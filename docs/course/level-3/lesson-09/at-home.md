# At home — Lesson 3.9

Do **one** of these.

## A. On your computer (Mac, Windows, or Linux)

1. Install qlcconsole: [Install](../../install.md).
2. Open `lesson-3-9-start.qlcc` again, and put a fault of your own into your copy: a song that doesn't Block, a hit nobody fires, or a group master at 0. Save it as `Lesson3-9-Home-yourname.qlcc` and swap with someone. Find theirs.

## B. On paper (no computer, or a Chromebook without Linux)

1. Write a troubleshooting card for the op, one side of an index card: the first three things to ask when a light is dark, in order, and where each is answered.
