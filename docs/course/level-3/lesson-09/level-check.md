# Level 3 check — run the rig through a preshow check and a song

*After Lesson 3.9, on the real rig, with Branson. Groups of 3: SM, board op, and the technician at the stage.*

## The task

1. **Open the show** on the console. Run **Review** and read the **Not ready**
   list. Fix what is wrong, or **Not a Problem…** it with a note. A blocker open at
   the end fails the check.
2. **The preshow:** Stage Power and the house lights come up as in Lesson 3.7;
   a focus call for the song's spots; every group master at full; the pad or
   the phone page set as the plan says.
3. **Run one song** from the show's cue list, with the SM calling every cue
   and the op taking it. Fire a hit once. One light is dark on purpose by the
   show's fault list; the op finds it with **Why is this on?**.
4. **Talk for two minutes:** what you checked before the house opened, and why
   in that order.

Everything it needs is in Lessons 3.1–3.9, on top of Level 2.

## How it's marked

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Checks** | Skipped the Review or Not ready | Read them; fixed some | Every blocker and warning fixed or accepted with a note | Notes that would help the next person; fixed the cheap things first |
| **Preshow** | Items missed | Done out of order | Complete and in order | Complete, in order, and each item said aloud |
| **Operation** | Cues missed or very late | Some early or late | Every cue on its call; the hit on the music | Clean calls; a fault found and fixed calmly |
| **Teamwork** | One person did it | Roles shared unevenly | Everyone had a role | Everyone can explain every step and why |

**Pass:** 3 or more in every row.
