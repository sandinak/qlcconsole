# Lesson 3.10 — Coming from Eos (optional)

For students who learned on an ETC Eos-family desk (Element, Ion, Gio) in
another programme. They bring a small Eos show into qlcconsole, check the
import's guesses about which channels are house lights, work lights and
relays, and map what they know from Eos onto the Show Tree. Any time after
[Lesson 3.7](../lesson-07/index.md).
