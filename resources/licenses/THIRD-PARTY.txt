qlcconsole: third-party software
================================

qlcconsole is a fork of Q Light Controller Plus (QLC+), licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt in this folder).

The installers (macOS DMG, Windows setup, Linux AppImage) also carry the
libraries below, each under its own licence. The full licence texts are in
this folder:

  LGPL-2.1.txt   GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1
  LGPL-3.0.txt   GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
  GPL-2.0.txt    GNU General Public License, version 2
  GPL-3.0.txt    GNU General Public License, version 3 (LGPL 3 is a set
                 of additional permissions on top of it)
  Apache-2.0.txt Apache License, Version 2.0 (qlcconsole itself)

Which libraries an installer carries depends on the platform; what each
licence covers:

Qt 6 (The Qt Company and contributors)                LGPL-3.0
  QtCore, QtGui, QtWidgets, QtNetwork, QtMultimedia, QtSvg, QtSerialPort,
  QtWebSockets, QtQml and the rest, and their plugins. Unmodified, linked
  dynamically: they can be replaced with your own build of the same Qt
  version. Source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/

FFmpeg (the FFmpeg developers)                         LGPL-2.1 or later
  libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswresample, libswscale, used by
  QtMultimedia for audio and video. Built WITHOUT --enable-gpl and without
  --enable-nonfree; linked dynamically, so they can be replaced.
  - macOS and Linux: the FFmpeg build Qt ships with its multimedia module
    (LGPL 2.1 or later).
  - Windows: BtbN's FFmpeg-Builds "lgpl-shared" release build (configured
    --enable-version3, so LGPL 3 or later), autobuild-2026-09-30-13-08,
    FFmpeg n9.0.2-17-g2a571b6068. Source:
    https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (build scripts) and
    https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git (FFmpeg, that commit).
  Source for every FFmpeg release: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

libftdi1 (Intra2net AG)                                LGPL-2.1
libsndfile (Erik de Castro Lopo)                       LGPL-2.1 or later
libmpg123, LAME                                        LGPL-2.1 or later
FLAC, Ogg, Vorbis, Opus (Xiph.Org)                     BSD-style

FFTW and libmad are GPL: they are used, unmodified and dynamically linked,
by the audio input spectrum (FFTW) and the MP3 decoder plugin (libmad), as
in upstream QLC+. Their source is at https://www.fftw.org/ and
https://www.underbit.com/products/mad/ (and every Linux distribution).

To get the exact source of any of these libraries as shipped, open an issue
at https://github.com/sandinak/qlcconsole/issues and it will be provided.
